Peace of Mind for the Whole Family

When your elderly parent or grandparent lives alone, a single question runs through your mind every day: “Are they okay?”

Phone calls go unanswered. You worry. They forget to charge the phone. You worry more. A missed call turns into panic.

Still Here solves this with a beautifully simple daily check-in that takes seconds — giving you peace of mind and keeping your loved one connected.

Everything Your Family Needs

Powerful features wrapped in the simplest interface possible. Built for seniors who aren’t tech-savvy and families who want to stay close.

✅

One-Tap Daily Check-In

Your loved one simply opens the app and you instantly know they’re safe. No complicated buttons, no typing required. Just open — done.

📸

Photo Sharing

Send photos from your camera or gallery directly to your senior family member. Add a caption like “Lake and sun” and brighten their whole day with a single tap.

🚨

Emergency Call Button

A large, clearly visible red emergency button is always accessible on the senior’s screen. One tap to call a pre-set emergency contact — no scrolling, no searching.

📞

Easy Calling & FaceTime

Call your loved one directly from the app with a single tap. Supports phone calls and FaceTime so you can see each other face to face, no setup needed.

👴

Senior-Friendly Design

Extra-large text, big colorful buttons, and a clutter-free interface. Your grandma doesn’t need to be tech-savvy — if she can tap a screen, she can use Still Here.

⏰

Check-In Status & Timestamps

See exactly when your loved one last checked in and when they viewed your photos. Know at a glance that everything is fine — or get alerted when it’s not.

👥

Multiple Family Contacts

Add several family members as contacts and emergency numbers. The whole family stays in the loop — share the responsibility and the connection.

🌟

Personalized Greeting

The app greets your loved one by name with a warm “Good Morning, Grandma!” along with today’s date. A small touch that makes all the difference.

How It Works

Set up in minutes, use every day with zero effort.

1

Download & Set Up

Install the app, add your senior’s profile, photo, and emergency contacts. Import contacts directly from your phone.

2

Give It to Your Loved One

Hand the phone to Grandma. The senior view is ultra-simple — a greeting, a contact to call, photos from family, and an emergency button.

3

Stay Connected Daily

Every time they open the app, you get a check-in. Send them photos, call them, and know they’re safe — every single day.
