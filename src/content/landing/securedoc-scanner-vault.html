Features

Everything you need. On your terms.

📷

Smart Document Scanner

Auto-detect edges, correct perspective, and digitize any document in seconds. Powered by Apple VisionKit.

🔒

Face ID & Touch ID Vault

Your documents are locked behind biometric authentication. No one gets in without your face or fingerprint.

🤖

AI-Powered OCR

Extract text from any scan instantly. Full-text search across all your documents, completely offline.

📝

Auto Summary & Language Detection

On-device AI reads your documents and generates smart summaries. Detects the language automatically.

🏷️

Tags & Categories

Organize receipts, contracts, invoices, and IDs with custom tags. Find anything in seconds.

💾

Flexible Backup & Sync

Back up your entire archive as a ZIP and export anywhere you want — or sync across your devices with your own Apple iCloud. Never a third-party server.
